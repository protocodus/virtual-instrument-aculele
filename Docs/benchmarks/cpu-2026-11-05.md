# CPU after 0.4.41, 2026-11-05

Five changes take work out of the engine without changing what it renders.
Each was checked on its own against 0.4.41 (`dd1e4bf`): a seeded random-MIDI
stream (note on and off, pitch bend, mod wheel, sustain, the string-channel
mode and the stroke keyswitches, 6 s, 256-sample blocks, the Piezo bus on
every other preset) through each of the seven factory presets at 44.1, 48 and
96 kHz, 21 streams; Shape and Wood changed every 0.7 s under fresh notes
through three presets at the same rates; and all seven preset demos. Every one
renders bit for bit as 0.4.41, under GCC 13.3 and under Clang 18 at
`-std=c++17 -O2 -fno-builtin`, the latter also with FMA contraction
(`-march=haswell`).

| Change | Where |
| --- | --- |
| The stretch force's interior cells read a ring stretch that does not wrap as plain offsets back from the newest sample, without a mask on each of their twelve reads per four cells. The wrapping case keeps the masked loop. | `sumAxialCells` |
| The capture filter lists the sections that are not flat. Five of its eight are `{1, 0, 0, 0, 0}` on every ukulele (the authored contour without the guitar contour, and the air and mid-band dips), and a flat section at rest passes its input through exactly. | `CaptureFilter::configure`, `render` |
| The body bank marks the groups of four modes none of which has a residue, and the runs they form, and advances those groups' states without forming their zero contributions. The states still advance, because a later Shape or Wood may sound the same slots (`sameBodyBank`). | `BodyBank::load`, `render` |
| The room forms four early reflections' cubic reads at once, each lane the one-tap sum's own arithmetic; the taps still join each channel's sum in tap order. | `RoomAmbience::prepare`, `step` |
| `ACULELE_ALWAYS_INLINE` now inlines under GCC as it already did under Clang: the string-loop step, its bridge velocity and the fixed derivative, about 22 calls a sample. | `AculeleEngine.h` |

The last change affects GCC builds only (this repository's tools and tests);
the Rack Extension compiles with Clang, which already inlined them.

Every demo is faster, by about as much as Callgrind predicts, but this shared
VM's run-to-run spread is about 5%, so take the wall-clock figures as
direction rather than size. What remains: the body bank (21-26% of a GCC
render), the stretch force's cell sums (14-19%, gathers that SSE2 can only do
a lane at a time), the bridge modes (11-12%) and the dispersion designs'
`atan2` calls (3%), all at or near the cost of their arithmetic with the
output held bit for bit.
