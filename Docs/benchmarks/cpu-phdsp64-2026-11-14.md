# CPU and Rack layout on 2026-11-14: 8-byte alignment, vectorised paths and inline steps

Engine 0.4.64 (`e8c6349`) against 0.4.65. Every change is exact: the seven
factory demos at 48 kHz and `Aculele.SampleRateLayoutParity` across 44.1, 48,
96 and 192 kHz (both the standard and `ACULELE_EXTENDED_SAMPLE_RATES=1`
layouts) render byte for byte as in 0.4.64.

## What was fixed and optimised

1. **8-byte heap alignment for Jukebox `phdsp64`.** `BridgeMode`, `BodyBank`
   and `RoomAmbience::arrivalWeights` carried `alignas(16)`, giving
   `AculeleEngine` and `Performer` 16-byte alignment requirements where the
   Rack runtime (`__BIGGEST_ALIGNMENT__ == 8`) guarantees only 8-byte heap
   alignment without over-aligned `operator new`. `alignof(AculeleEngine)` and
   `alignof(Performer)` are now 8, enforced by `static_assert` and by
   `-fnew-alignment=8 -fno-aligned-allocation -Wover-aligned` on
   `AculeleDSPCxx17`. Every GNU vector type carries `aligned(4), may_alias`
   (`float`) or `aligned(8), may_alias` (`double`).
2. **No `-fno-builtin` library call or per-sample closure call.**
   - `StringLoop::advance` called `std::abs` on the delay target check, which
     emitted a `_fabs` call on every delay-slewing sample under `-fno-builtin`;
     it now uses `exact::abs`.
   - `renderPiezo` called its `clipCorner` lambda out of line twice on every
     sample even when unclipped; the rail-crossing check is now inline and
     `piezoClipCorner` and `piezoDiodeDump` are `ACULELE_NOINLINE`.
   - `AculeleEngine::process` wrapped `FixedDerivative` in a non-inlined
     closure under `-O2`, emitting six to seven calls per sample; the normal
     path now calls `ACULELE_ALWAYS_INLINE FixedDerivative::process` directly.
3. **Vectorised stereo capture, room FDN and ARM64 stretch sum.**
   - `sumAxialCells` now enables its 4-wide `Floats` path on ARM64 alongside
     x86-64, keeping scalar-order `addSquares` so rounding is bit-identical.
   - `BodyBank::CaptureFilter::render` advances left and right channels
     together in a 2-lane `double` vector with bitwise `< 1e-30` flush while
     preserving intermediate `float` rounding.
   - `RoomAmbience::step` advances its 8-line FDN absorption, `< 1e-20f`
     bitwise flush and 8-point Hadamard feedback mix in two 4-wide float
     vectors, with constant lane indexing removing vector stack spills in
     `RoomAmbience::step` and `BodyBank::render`.
4. **Immediate-offset member layout, cache guards and loop micro-paths.**
   - Hot scalars in `Performer`, `AculeleEngine`, `Voice`, `BodyBank`,
     `BridgeLoad`, `RoomAmbience`, `ContactTravel` and `RepluckArrivals` sit
     ahead of multi-kilobyte rings so per-sample loads/stores stay within
     single-instruction immediate offsets.
   - `StringLoop::readDelay` takes a contiguous four-tap read when `idx0 >= 2`,
     `StringLoop::write` combines finite and `< 1e-30f` checks into one
     unsigned exponent-range test, `StringLoop::advanceIntrinsicCoefficients`
     moves its 112-byte pointer table out of `StringLoop::advance`,
     `renderStringAxialForce` checks its exact-bit design cache inline before
     calling `designStringAxialForce`, and `captureMix_` glides only the three
     active ukulele capture routes (`StereoMic`, `Piezo`, `MonoMic`) until
     settled.
