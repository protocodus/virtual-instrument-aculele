# Evenness across shapes, woods, Picking and strings, 2026-11-05

The owner asked for every body, wood and picking style to be more consistent
in volume, tone and resonance, and for the ukulele to sound smoother and more
even across its strings. This is what was measured at 0.4.39, what 0.4.40
changed and what is left.

`Tools/AculeleEvenness.cpp` plays every string from open to the 12th fret, each
note alone after a reset at velocity 90, in each of the 48 cells (three Shapes,
four Woods, four Pickings), on nylon in Standard tuning through the stereo pair
at Concert Strum's other controls; its header gives the protocol. It reports
each string's mean level (K-weighted, first second) and brightness (share of
power above 2.5 kHz, first 0.3 s) against the four strings' mean, the cell's
mean brightness, the notes' level SD about a line in pitch, and how far apart
one pitch plays on different strings. `AculeleEvenness` built against 0.4.39's source and against 0.4.40's
gives the two columns below.

## Level across shapes, woods and Picking was already even

`LevelMatchData.h` brings typical playing (`Tools/ReasonMeter.h`) to -9 VU in
every cell and on each Capture, and `Aculele.Levels` holds each preset within
0.5 dB of it and each Capture and Picking within 1.5 dB. At 0.4.39 every cell
on every Capture read within 0.9 dB of -9 VU. Nothing there needed changing.

## Tone across woods and Picking differs on purpose

The Pickings' brightness differs by design: over all notes and cells, Thumb's
pad plays 6 dB darker than Finger and Pick 2 dB brighter, at 0.4.39 and 0.4.40
alike, as the contacts they
model do. At 0.4.40 the woods' brightness differs by 2-3 dB on the soprano,
3-4 on the concert and 5-7 on the tenor, the graduated tops of 0.4.36 that
were made to be heard. Neither is a fault to even out.

## The nylon C string was the one string out of line

| Mean over 12 shapes and woods | 0.4.39 | 0.4.40 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C string's brightness against the four strings' mean, Finger | -16.2 dB | -1.9 dB |
| the same, Pick | -10.6 | +2.4 |
| the same, Thumb | -18.3 | -4.4 |
| the same, Strum | -16.1 | -2.1 |
| C string's level against the four strings' mean, Finger / Pick / Thumb / Strum | -1.4 / -4.5 / -1.0 / -1.3 dB | -0.5 / -3.1 / -0.3 / -0.5 dB |

Over all 48 cells:

| | 0.4.39 | 0.4.40 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| brightest string against the dullest, mean (worst cell) | 25.1 dB (38.2) | 9.7 dB (21.5) |
| loudest string against the quietest, mean (worst cell) | 4.2 dB (9.3) | 3.3 dB (7.0) |
| note-to-note level SD about a line in pitch | 2.59 dB | 2.41 dB |
| one pitch on different strings, loudest against quietest | 3.49 dB | 3.01 dB |

At 0.4.39 the C string's brightness sat 4.8-22 dB under the four strings'
mean in every cell.
D'Addario's unwound nylon C is the stiffest string of the set (inharmonicity
B 5e-4 to 8e-4 over C4-D#4), and the bending loss of Woodhouse and Lynch-Aird,
eta_E B n^2 / (1 + B n^2), grows with that stiffness. Five sampled ukuleles'
C strings read B 1.3e-4 to 4.6e-4 and lose 10-30 dB of their power above
2.5 kHz from a note's first 20 ms to its 300-600 ms, where the nylon C loses
58-67 dB; nylon's G, E and A lie inside the recordings' range on both
([benchmark](sample-pack-benchmark-2026-10-29.md)). Worth's fluorocarbon C
(.0291 in) reads B 2.6e-4 and loses 33 dB.

0.4.40 strings the nylon sets with that fluorocarbon C, a hybrid set: G, E
and A stay D'Addario's nylon, and the fluorocarbon sets are unchanged. The force law keeps EJ65C as sold for its reference
tension, so no other string's force moves. The C string's notes now lose 5 dB
less between their first 0.35 s and 1.0-1.3 s, and the other strings' about
1 dB less, as the open C rings under them. The woods' brightness differences
above grew by 0.1-1.7 dB, since the C string now has top end for the body to
colour.

The open C also rings sympathetically longer under the other strings, as the
Fluorocarbon set's C already did: under C#5 on the G string, the open C's
octave at 524 Hz stands 9 dB under the note, against 18 dB with the nylon C
(14 dB on the Fluorocarbon set). It sits a semitone from that note, inside the
pitch tracker of `Aculele.Vibrato`, which moved its full-depth note to D5 on
the G string's 7th fret.

## What is left

- **The G string plays 1.2-2.8 dB above the four strings' mean on the
  microphones** (mean over shapes and woods by Picking, Strum lowest, Pick
  highest).
  Removing the bridge's moment input takes about 0.8 dB of that away on six
  cells; the rest is not traced. Nothing was changed.
- **Under Pick the C string still plays 3.1 dB under the mean.** The plectrum
  plucks at 0.40 of the Finger's distance from the bridge; at the Finger's
  distance the gap closes to under 1 dB, but that takes away what makes Pick a
  pick. Nothing was changed.
- **The A string dips at C5-D#5 on the concert**, where the body radiates
  least between its first top mode and the 684 Hz mode. Nothing was changed.
- Single notes rise only about 0.1 dB per velocity step between 70 and 110, as
  the promo work found: the stroke's force follows velocity linearly. That is
  the velocity curve, not evenness, and was not changed here.
