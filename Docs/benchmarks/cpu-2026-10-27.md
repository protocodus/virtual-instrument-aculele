# CPU for 0.3.0, 2026-10-27

Four changes take work out of the engine without changing what it renders.
Every README demo, rendered as raw floats at 44.1, 48, 96 and 192 kHz in
256- and 37-sample blocks, is bit-identical before and after them. The one
difference found is a construction change while sounding: soprano to concert
differs by -75 dBFS at its peak (-101 dB RMS re the signal), because modes the
soprano leaves silent now start from rest when the concert sounds them.

| Change | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Body modes at or above 0.46 fs have no residues and add exactly zero; the bank stops at the last mode that sounds. For the factory presets at 48 kHz that skips 64 of a soprano's 160 modes, 42-45 of a concert's and 32-35 of a tenor's. | `BodyBank::load`, `BodyBank::render` |
| The note-on dispersion fit computes its fixed second all-pass section's phase once per fit instead of in every evaluation, and its residuals apart from its Jacobian, so a rejected step costs no Jacobian. | `refineDispersionDesign` |
| The bridge's heave and rock sections, which share their coefficients, sit side by side, so the pair steps with whole loads; the residues are held in double. | `BridgeLoad::advanceModes` |
| The key-up brush of a wound string, which no ukulele string has, is gone with its state; its two random draws stay so every later draw is unchanged. | `startReleaseNoise` |

## Measurements

Release build, GCC 13.3 `-O2`, a 4-core Intel Xeon at 2.80 GHz VM. Before is
`9ab46c0` (0.3.0's sound), after is the commit that adds this report.

`RenderAculelePresetDemos --cpu` (48 kHz, 256-sample blocks, median of 3),
before and after alternating over three rounds, median of the rounds:

| Demo | Before (x realtime) | After | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 Concert Strum | 0.0927 | 0.0787 | -15% |
| 02 Soprano D Tuning | 0.0913 | 0.0767 | -16% |
| 03 Tenor Fluorocarbon Fingerstyle | 0.0853 | 0.0727 | -15% |
| 04 Low G Tenor | 0.0879 | 0.0701 | -20% |
| 05 Soprano Thumb | 0.0850 | 0.0712 | -16% |
| 06 Tenor Picked Melody | 0.0857 | 0.0647 | -25% |
| 07 Concert Piezo Stage | 0.0855 | 0.0707 | -17% |
| Mean | 0.0876 | 0.0721 | -18% |

The worst single block is the note-on fit. On Soprano Thumb it went from 129% to
87% of the 5.33 ms block (median of the three rounds); the other demos' worst blocks are within the run-to-run noise.

Instructions executed under Callgrind, one render of the demo at 48 kHz in
256-sample blocks, which do not vary from run to run:

| Demo | Before | After | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 Concert Strum | 9.42 G | 8.45 G | -10% |
| 02 Soprano D Tuning | 9.57 G | 8.35 G | -13% |

The wall-clock saving is larger than the instruction count's; what accounts
for the difference was not measured.

These are development-machine figures, not Reason or DAW CPU.
