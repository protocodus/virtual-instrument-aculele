# CPU after 0.4.38, 2026-11-03

Two changes take work out of the engine without changing what it renders. A
seeded random-MIDI stream (note on and off, pitch bend, mod wheel, sustain and
the string-channel mode, 6 s, 256-sample blocks) through each of the seven
factory presets at 44.1, 48 and 96 kHz renders bit for bit as 0.4.38 with both
of them, 21 streams in all.

| Change | Where |
| --- | --- |
| The room's early reflections read from a ring held twice, one capacity apart, so a tap is a pointer offset instead of a wrapped index, and each arrival's four weighted samples are four consecutive loads. The eight-line mixing matrix is three butterfly stages written out instead of a loop over strides. | `RoomAmbience::step`, `roomHadamard` |
| A string whose delay has reached its target (the usual case between note changes) skips the delay follower: its state, progress and target are already the exact values the follower would write back. | `StringLoop::advance` |

## Measurements

Release build, GCC 13.3 `-O3 -DNDEBUG`, a 4-core Intel Xeon VM at 2.10 GHz.
Before is `e257081` (0.4.38); after is the commit that adds this report.

Instructions executed under Callgrind, which do not vary from run to run: one
render of the demo at 48 kHz in 256-sample blocks, the first 6 s.

| Demo | Before (G instructions) | After | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 Concert Strum | 6.076 | 5.889 | -3.1% |
| 03 Tenor Fluorocarbon Fingerstyle | 4.718 | 4.523 | -4.1% |

Wall clock does not resolve it. The two builds alternating on one pinned core,
median of 7 renders of 8 s of the same two demos:

| Rate | Demo | Before (s) | After | Ratio |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 48 kHz | 01 | 0.698 | 0.693 | 0.99 |
| 48 kHz | 03 | 0.501 | 0.509 | 1.02 |
| 96 kHz | 01 | 1.349 | 1.410 | 1.05 |
| 96 kHz | 03 | 1.051 | 1.009 | 0.96 |

The run-to-run spread of this shared VM is about 5%, larger than the 3-4% the
instruction counts show, so no wall-clock gain is claimed. What remains is
spread over the body bank (22% of a 96 kHz render of demo 01), the bridge modes
(10%) and the stiff-string dispersion design, whose `atan2` calls are 11% of
that render. The design is already prepared ahead of its note a start at a
time (`prepareDispersion`), so it adds to the total but not to a note-on block.
